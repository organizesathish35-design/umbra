Less, better,

The reason

We got tired of throwing things away

UMBRA started because our founder counted the clothes she actually wore in a year. It came to eleven. The rest were bought on colour, worn twice, and quietly given away.

So we built the opposite of that: a fixed wardrobe of fourteen pieces in two colours that cannot clash, made from fibre that improves rather than pills, in quantities small enough that nothing ends up in an outlet.

What we hold to

Three rules, no exceptions

01

Two colours, permanently

Black and white, plus whatever the fibre is before we dye it. No seasonal palette, no trend colour, nothing that dates the piece you bought last year.

02

Natural fibre only

Cotton, wool, linen, silk. The one exception is a recycled polyester in the Cinder trouser, where the drape genuinely needs it — and we say so on the page.

03

One batch per piece

Four hundred units, then the pattern goes in the drawer. It means we sell out, and it means we never discount to clear a warehouse.

How we got here

Five years, slowly

2021

One tee, one kitchen table

The Zero tee, cut in a run of forty and sold to friends. Eleven toiles before the shoulder was right.

2022

Porto

We found the knitter who still runs compact-spun cotton on old machines and agreed to take their minimum.

2023

Black, three passes

Six months and a lot of wasted cloth working out how to reach a black that does not go brown.

2024

The studio

A single room in Hackney with a cutting table and good north light. Every sample since has been made in it.

2026

Drop I

Fourteen pieces, finally finished. Released in one batch, exactly as intended from the start.
