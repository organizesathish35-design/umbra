03 — How it is made

Four steps, none of them fast

01 / Fibre

We buy the cloth first

Long-staple organic cotton from Porto, undyed merino from Hawick, flax from Normandy. The mill is chosen before the pattern exists — the cloth decides what the garment can be.

02 / Colour

Three passes to black

A single dye bath gives you a black that turns brown in eighteen months. We run three, and hold the batch back if the depth is off by a shade.

03 / Cut

One block, revised eleven times

Every piece shares a shoulder and a hem so the collection layers cleanly. The Zero tee took eleven toiles before the shoulder seam sat where we wanted it.

04 / Finish

Washed twice, pressed once

Garments are washed before they ship so they arrive at their final dimensions. What you take out of the box is what it will be in three years.
